# Business and impact hypotheses

This is a proposed model, not a commercial result.

The buyer gets a Bengali comparison workflow and understandable evidence. An assisted-service institution could pay for permitted workflow volume, support and deployment while basic guidance remains accessible. No payment feature, partner contract, price, revenue or willingness-to-pay study is implemented/claimed.

Cost drivers include static/OCR delivery, Worker/database activity, optional AI calls, support, domain review and authorized-data integration. Browser OCR and bounded optional AI can reduce server work and cap some variable use. Unit economics must be measured before a pricing decision.

A pilot should evaluate review time, missed discrepancies, false mismatch alerts caused by extraction, correction effort, comprehension, successful completion and cost per completed comparison. It must not infer money saved or fraud prevented from synthetic fixture counts.

Countrywide expansion would require lawful authoritative interfaces, regional terminology/document coverage, assisted/offline-friendly access, monitoring, governance and professional review. None follows automatically from deploying a serverless prototype.
